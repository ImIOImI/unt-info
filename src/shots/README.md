Screenshots and diagrams. `battlefield.png` is the map panel cropped out of
the Legion 1 operation-order graphic, which is where the zone boundaries and
the Undercellar spawn points come from.

The building labels on it are baked-in English. That is on purpose: the page
names every building again in the reader's own language right beside the
image, and re-lettering the art in nine languages would go stale the first
time the map changes.
